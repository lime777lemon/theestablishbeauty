Shipping

Store products are purchased from the linked selling business, such as the EMR-TEK official store. Referral links on pages such as beauty and fashion are purchases from the business linked through an affiliate service such as A8.net. Origin, delivery time, shipping fees, delivery area, duties, import tax, and clearance fees follow that business’s disclosures.

We do not ship products, and we do not set delivery times or shipping fees. Before you buy, check the shipping terms on the seller’s page.

Returns & refunds

Returns, cancellation, defects, wrong items, and refunds are set by the selling business. The return window, who pays return shipping, and whether an exchange is available also follow that business’s disclosures.

We do not accept returns, and we do not provide a return address, exchange, refund, or replacement. Contact the selling business for those steps. The contact form on this site is for questions about what this site displays.

Payment

Payment methods, timing, processing fees, and billing currency are set by the selling business. Card details and other payment data are processed by that business or its payment provider.

We do not take payment for the products.

Pricing, tax & fees

The price, consumption tax, shipping, duties, and other fees that apply are those shown by the selling business where you check out. Prices and terms on this site are for reference. The seller’s confirmation screen controls.

We do not set the seller’s price as our own selling price.
