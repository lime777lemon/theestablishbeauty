Shipping policy

Transparency (US → Japan)

Routes from North America (e.g. the US) to Japan involve international transport and customs, so delivery usually takes longer than domestic purchases. Please review before ordering (estimates only, not guaranteed):

  • Transit time (estimate): After warehouse dispatch, allow about 7–14 business days to addresses in Japan. Peak seasons, weather, customs congestion, address errors, or redelivery may extend this. On-screen “dispatch estimate (e.g. 2–5 business days)” refers to time until the warehouse ships, not international transit.
  • Customs duties: Imports into Japan may incur duties, import consumption tax, and clearance fees. These are usually not included in product price or shipping and may be customer-paid per customs/carrier notice. Rates depend on declared value and classification; we cannot guarantee exact amounts in advance. Duties may not apply in some cases.
  • Tracking: When the carrier provides a tracking number, tracking is usually available after dispatch. Numbers and URLs are sent by email. Some services have no tracking or delayed updates for part of the journey.

Origin & international delivery

Items ship from our overseas locations, suppliers, or partner warehouses to Japan and other destinations. “Dispatch estimate (e.g. 2–5 business days)” is business days until shipment from the warehouse and excludes international transit, customs, and last-mile delivery.

Delivery time

International delivery may be delayed by carrier capacity, weather, customs backlog, holidays, address issues, or redelivery. When tracking is available, we notify you after dispatch (by email or account page where offered).

Shipping fees & regions

Fees vary by order, destination, and promotions. Free shipping thresholds, if any, are shown on product and cart pages. Remote areas may incur surcharges.

Duties, import tax & clearance

Cross-border shipments may incur duties, import tax, clearance, and broker fees payable by the customer under local law. These are often separate from product price and shipping. Amounts vary; we may not be able to quote exact totals in advance.

Returns & refunds

See also our Terms of Service. Below is an example for import e-commerce and international shipping (define exact periods and fees for production use).

  • Customer-initiated returns/cancellation: If unopened and unused, contact us within 14 days of delivery (example) via Contact. After approval we provide a return address. International return shipping, duties, and fees are generally customer-paid. Opened or used items may be refused for hygiene or product reasons.
  • Defects, wrong items, damage: Contact us promptly with photos. After verification we may offer exchange, refund, or reshipment. International returns/exchanges may take 2–4 weeks round trip.
  • Refunds: When approved, refunds follow your payment provider’s timeline (days to weeks). Bank refunds may net of fees.
  • Cancellation: Possible before shipment; after shipment, returns policy applies.

Payment

Available methods are those offered at checkout (e.g. credit card and other methods we specify). See the checkout page when available.

  • Timing: Payment is usually taken when the order is placed or when we accept it. Other methods follow their own rules.
  • Security: Card data is processed by PCI-compliant payment providers; we do not store full card numbers directly.
  • Currency & FX: If display currency differs from your card’s billing currency, your issuer’s FX rate and fees on the charge date apply.

Pricing, tax & fees

Prices are shown in Japanese yen (JPY) following each page’s tax-inclusive or tax-exclusive policy. Import inventory and FX may require price changes without notice; the price at order confirmation applies.

  • Included in product price: The item itself. Shipping, payment fees, duties, and import charges may be extra or billed separately.
  • Consumption tax: Display follows domestic rules (tax-inclusive or tax-exclusive as labeled). Tax-free offers, if any, state eligibility on product pages.
  • Coupons & campaigns: Terms of each offer apply, including stacking rules.
