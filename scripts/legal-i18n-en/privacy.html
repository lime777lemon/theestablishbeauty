1. Introduction

EMR-TEK (“we,” “us”) considers proper handling of personal data essential when conducting import e-commerce and dropshipping (including fulfillment via suppliers, warehouses, and logistics partners after an order is placed). This policy explains how we handle information collected through this site.

2. Information we collect

We may collect:

  • Name, email, phone, shipping address, and similar data for orders, inquiries, and account registration
  • Payment-related information (processed by payment providers; we generally do not store full card numbers)
  • Site usage data (access logs, cookies and similar identifiers, device information)
  • Inquiry content and records related to returns or exchanges

3. Purposes of use

We use collected information to:

  • Sell products, process payment, ship orders, and communicate about orders
  • Share with suppliers and carriers as needed for dropshipping fulfillment
  • Provide customer support, prevent misuse, and comply with law
  • Analyze service improvement in aggregated or de-identified form where applicable
  • Marketing within legal limits (with opt-out where offered)

4. Third parties & processors

We may entrust handling to carriers, payment providers, hosting providers, email services, and others as needed for dropshipping. We may disclose information when required by law or for legitimate reasons within necessary scope.

5. International transfers

If servers, suppliers, or logistics sites are located abroad, your information may be processed outside your country. We take appropriate safeguards as required by applicable law.

6. Cookies

We may use cookies and local storage to improve experience, maintain carts, and analyze usage. You can disable cookies in your browser, but some features may not work.

7. Access, correction & deletion

We will respond within a reasonable scope to requests to access, correct, restrict use of, or delete personal data. Contact us via the contact form or the email below.

8. Security

We implement reasonable organizational, technical, and physical safeguards against leakage, loss, or damage of personal data.

9. Policy changes

We may revise this policy due to legal or business changes. The revised version takes effect when posted on this page.
