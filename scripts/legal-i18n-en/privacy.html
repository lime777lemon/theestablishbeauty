If servers, suppliers, or logistics sites are located abroad, your information may be processed outside your country. We take appropriate safeguards as required by applicable law.

6. Cookies

We may use cookies and local storage to improve experience, maintain carts, and analyze usage. When Google AdSense auto ads are shown, Google may use cookies to deliver and measure those ads. Referral links on pages such as beauty and fashion, including affiliate services such as A8.net, may use cookies set by those services or the linked businesses to measure clicks and results. You can disable cookies in your browser, but some features may not work.

7. Instagram and Meta

Browsing this site does not require an Instagram or Facebook login. We use the Instagram API from Meta Platforms, Inc. (“Meta”) only when we connect our own Instagram professional account to this site.

When that connection is made, we may receive and store the following on our server:

  • An access token (kept on the server and not shown in the browser)
  • The Instagram user ID and username
  • The permissions granted, and a business ID returned by Meta

We use this information to confirm the connection and, after we turn posting on, to publish text and images to the connected Instagram account. We do not use it to collect Instagram accounts of site visitors. When we publish, we send the text, images, and destination account ID to Meta.

Meta’s handling of data is described in the Meta Privacy Policy. The connection can be removed in Instagram or Facebook settings. To ask us to delete a token we still hold, use the contact form or the email below. We keep a token until the connection is removed or the token is replaced.
